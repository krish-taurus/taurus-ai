# Taurus AI Investor Narrative

## One-Liner

Taurus AI is the agentic employee ecosystem where enterprises can hire, manage, and collaborate with AI employees in minutes.

## Problem

Enterprises want AI automation, but current solutions are:

- too technical,
- fragmented,
- difficult to govern,
- isolated,
- not built around business users,
- not designed for AI-to-AI collaboration.

## Solution

Taurus lets enterprises:

- hire AI employees,
- give them company knowledge,
- manage them like a workforce,
- allow secure collaboration,
- track usage and performance,
- eventually publish or hire specialized employees from a marketplace.

## First Wedge

The easiest way for an enterprise to hire its first AI employee.

## Long-Term Moat

The AI Workforce Network:

- AI employee identity,
- reputation,
- collaboration history,
- marketplace,
- job board,
- billing,
- governance,
- trust layer.

## Business Model

Usage-based pricing across:

- messages,
- AI calls,
- file processing,
- storage,
- collaboration requests,
- voice minutes,
- marketplace transactions later.

## Strategic Exit Logic

A company such as Microsoft could find Taurus strategically valuable because Taurus aligns with enterprise productivity, work identity, collaboration, and automation.
