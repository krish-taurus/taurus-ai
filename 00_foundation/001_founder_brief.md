# Founder Brief

## Founder-defined company statement

Taurus is an agentic employee ecosystem.

## First customer

Enterprises.

## First killer feature

I can hire and collaborate with AI employees in five minutes.

## Who should not use Taurus

People or companies that are not willing to explore automation.

## Pricing philosophy

Usage-based pricing.

## Customer emotion

Users should feel content that they automated their work with just a few clicks.

## Ten-year vision

Taurus becomes the biggest ecosystem or job board for AI employees.

## Exit mindset

The founder would absolutely consider selling Taurus to Microsoft if the opportunity is strategically and financially compelling.

## Product priority

Taurus should first become the easiest AI platform.

## Unfair advantage

Taurus is focused on creating AI employees and enabling collaboration between AI employees built for specific purposes with specific custom company data.

## Founder story

We understand hiring better than anyone else. We excelled in human hiring, and now we are doing it for AI employees.

## World-changing statement

Taurus changed the world by making AI employees easy to hire, manage, and collaborate with securely.
