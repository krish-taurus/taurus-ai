# Taurus Constitution v1

## Mission

Empower every enterprise to hire, manage, govern, and collaborate with trusted AI employees as easily as hiring human employees.

## Vision

Build the world's largest AI Workforce Network, where companies can securely create, deploy, discover, hire, and collaborate with AI employees.

## Category

Taurus AI is an agentic employee ecosystem.

It is not merely:

- a chatbot platform,
- a voice agent platform,
- a workflow automation tool,
- an LLM wrapper.

Taurus is the enterprise layer around AI employees.

## North Star

A non-technical enterprise user should be able to hire a useful AI employee in five minutes.

## Product Principles

### 1. Easiest first

Taurus must become the easiest AI employee platform before it becomes the most powerful one.

### 2. Hiring, not configuration

Users should feel like they are hiring an employee, not configuring software.

### 3. No prompt engineering for business users

Users should not need to write prompts. Taurus converts business intent into structured Employee DNA.

### 4. Enterprise trust from day one

Security, organization isolation, permissions, and auditability are mandatory from the start.

### 5. Collaboration is the moat

Individual AI employees are useful. A trusted ecosystem of collaborating AI employees is defensible.

### 6. Voice is a channel

Voice is important, but the product is not a voice bot. Voice is one channel through which AI employees work.

### 7. Data belongs to the customer

Customer knowledge, files, conversations, and operational data belong to the customer.

### 8. Every AI employee has identity

Each AI employee must have persistent identity, role, status, permissions, DNA, knowledge scope, and activity history.

### 9. Humans stay in control

Sensitive actions require permissions, guardrails, and human approval policies.

### 10. Automation should reduce stress

The user should feel content, not overwhelmed.

## Engineering Principles

- Build modularly.
- Avoid premature microservices.
- Use strong internal boundaries.
- Version important configuration.
- Log important actions.
- Design for enterprise adoption.
- Stay provider-agnostic.
- Keep the MVP shippable.

## AI Behavior Principles

- Admit uncertainty.
- Ask clarifying questions when needed.
- Do not hallucinate company facts.
- Cite sources when possible.
- Escalate when confidence is low.
- Never expose private company knowledge without permission.

## Security Principles

- Tenant isolation.
- Least privilege.
- Explicit consent for sharing.
- Audit every important action.
- Encrypt sensitive data.
- No silent cross-company knowledge leakage.
